Pfumvudza

Standard plot

A Pfumvudza plot is about 39 m × 16 m (roughly 1/16 hectare).

Steps

  1. Mark out the plot and dig planting basins in rows.
  2. Add manure or compost to each basin.
  3. Plant seed and cover lightly.
  4. Mulch the whole plot with crop residue or grass to keep moisture and cut weeds.
  5. Top-dress and weed on time; rotate crops each season.

Confirm basin spacing and input quantities with your Agritex officer.
